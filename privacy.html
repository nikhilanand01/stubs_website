Privacy Policy

Last updated February 8, 2026

Introduction

Stubs is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Data Controller

Stubs (independent app, not yet incorporated)
Based in New York, New York, USA
Contact: contactstubs@gmail.com

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address and username when you create an account
  • Concert Data: Information about concerts you track, including artist names, venue details, dates, and any notes you add
  • Usage Data: Basic information derived from your activity in the app (such as number of concerts logged or venues visited)

How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation under the following legal bases:

  • Provide and maintain the Stubs app functionality
  • Generate statistics and insights about your concert history
  • Sync your data across your devices
  • Improve the app experience based on how users interact with features
  • Communicate with you about app updates or support issues

Data Storage and Security

Your data is securely stored using a third-party database service. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no system is completely secure.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party services that help us operate the app
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

International Data Transfers

As we are based in the United States, your data may be transferred to and processed outside of the European Economic Area (EEA). Where this occurs, we take appropriate safeguards to protect your data.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Export your concert data
  • Object to or restrict how we process your data

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

Children’s Privacy

Stubs is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the “Last Updated” date at the top of this policy.
